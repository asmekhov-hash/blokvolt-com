Hardware partners for the Balkans.

We are building the home-charging service for apartment buildings in Serbia, and we are selecting the wallbox suppliers we will standardise on. This page is what our procurement side looks like, so you can decide in two minutes whether it is worth a quotation.

Send a quotation What BlokVolt does

BlokVolt is the service layer of home charging: we sell an installed, metered, documented and supported charging point to someone who lives in an apartment building — not a box in a carton. The hardware is one component of that, but it is the component our customer looks at, so we care about it more than the price list suggests.

We are early, and we would rather be plain about it: volumes today are pilot-scale, growing with a national fleet that roughly doubles every twenty months. We would rather say that than quote a number we cannot stand behind. What we can offer a supplier is a market where roughly ten companies sell hardware and none of them sell the service layer, an installation model built on electricians paid per completed job, and a customer relationship that continues monthly after the sale — which is exactly the kind of channel that produces repeat orders instead of one-off tenders.

What we buy

  • AC wallboxes, 11 kW three-phase (7.4 kW single-phase variants of interest)
  • Type 2 socket and tethered-cable versions
  • RFID or app authorisation
  • Dynamic load balancing / current limiting
  • OCPP 1.6J or newer, open backend
  • Reinforced-socket kits for the entry-level package

Non-negotiable

  • CE marking and EU-conformant documentation
  • Compatibility with an external MID-certified sub-meter, or MID metering built in
  • IP54 or better, realistic winter operating range
  • Spare parts available in Europe
  • Warranty terms in writing, including the RMA path

Worth mentioning

  • Rebadging or OEM branding, and the MOQ that unlocks it
  • DDP pricing into Serbia, customs code, lead time
  • Existing distributor in Serbia or the region, if any
  • Firmware update policy and backend API access
  • Marketing or demo-unit support for a pilot
